# Code of Conduct

The `jol-security` repository exists to support security, compliance, and operational integrity for the Journey of Life platform. We expect every participant to behave professionally, communicate clearly, and contribute in a way that supports safe, respectful, and accountable collaboration.

We are committed to providing an environment free from harassment, intimidation, discrimination, retaliation, and abusive behavior. This standard applies to maintainers, contributors, reviewers, contractors, and any other person interacting with this repository.

## Expected Behavior

We expect all participants to:

- Act with professionalism, restraint, and respect
- Focus on the technical issue, control, or risk under discussion
- Give constructive feedback without personal attacks
- Protect privacy and confidentiality
- Raise concerns through the correct governance and escalation channels
- Accept moderation decisions and enforcement actions in good faith

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or bullying
- Discriminatory comments or exclusionary conduct
- Personal attacks, insults, or degrading language
- Doxxing, disclosure of private information, or misuse of confidential details
- Deliberate disruption of review, audit, or incident processes
- Repeatedly ignoring moderator instructions or governance rules
- Using repository discussions to pressure, shame, or retaliate against another person

## Professional Conduct in This Repository

Because this repository supports security and compliance work, participants must keep discussions accurate, concise, and evidence-based. Speculation should be labeled clearly, and unsupported claims should not be presented as fact.

### When Raising a Concern

- Describe the issue clearly
- Include relevant context or evidence
- Avoid emotional or inflammatory language
- Use the appropriate issue, PR, or escalation path

### When Reviewing Changes

- Critique the change, not the person
- Keep comments relevant to security, compliance, maintainability, or operational risk
- Avoid sarcasm, ridicule, or public pressure

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers and designated governance owners may remove, edit, hide, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct. They may also restrict access, close issues, block pull requests, or escalate matters to organizational leadership when necessary.

Decisions will be made with a focus on safety, fairness, and preservation of the repository's integrity. This enforcement approach is consistent with the [Contributor Covenant's moderation principles](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

### Enforcement Actions

| Action              | When Applied                                      |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Warning             | First minor violation, good-faith correction       |
| Content removal     | Inappropriate comments, confidential data exposure |
| Temporary ban       | Repeated violations or deliberate disruption        |
| Permanent ban       | Severe or persistent misconduct                   |
| Escalation          | Legal, HR, or executive involvement required       |

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this Code of Conduct, report it through the repository's defined security or governance channel. Reports should be handled confidentially and only shared with the minimum number of people needed to assess and resolve the matter.

### Contact

| Channel                     | Contact                          |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Primary conduct contact     | conduct@journeyoflife.org        |
| Security-related conduct    | security@journeyoflife.org       |
| Escalation                  | ciso@journeyoflife.org           |

### Reports Should Include

- A concise description of the incident
- Approximate date and time
- Relevant links, screenshots, or references
- Names or handles of involved participants, if appropriate
- Any immediate safety or access concerns

## Confidential Handling

Reports, moderation discussions, and related records may contain sensitive or personal information. Such material must be treated as **confidential** and handled in accordance with the repository's privacy, security, and retention rules.

Do not repost moderation records publicly unless explicitly authorized.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- Issues, pull requests, and code review comments
- Repository discussions and maintainer communications
- Related governance, security, and compliance collaboration

It applies to all contributors regardless of role, seniority, location, or affiliation.

## Review and Updates

This document should be reviewed periodically to ensure it remains enforceable, clear, and aligned with the repository's governance model. Any revision should be approved through normal change control per [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
