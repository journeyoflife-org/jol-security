# Asset Management Policy

**Policy ID**: POL-AM-001
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Review Cycle**: Annually
**Owner**: Security Lead
**Classification**: Internal

## Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for identifying, classifying, and managing information assets throughout their lifecycle.

## Scope

Applies to all information assets owned, operated, or maintained by the Journey of Life organization.

## Policy Statements

### 1. Asset Inventory

- A current inventory of all information assets shall be maintained
- Each asset shall have an assigned owner responsible for its management
- Asset inventory shall be reviewed and updated quarterly

### 2. Asset Classification

- All assets shall be classified per `compliance/data-classification.md`
- Classification levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted
- Asset owners are responsible for assigning and reviewing classifications

### 3. Acceptable Use

- Assets shall only be used for authorized business purposes
- Personal use of organizational assets requires written approval
- All use must comply with applicable laws and regulations

### 4. Asset Lifecycle

- Assets shall be tracked from acquisition through disposal
- Disposal of assets containing data must follow secure sanitization procedures
- Records of asset disposal shall be retained for 3 years

### 5. Return of Assets

- All organizational assets must be returned upon termination of employment or contract
- Return shall be completed within 5 business days of departure
- Asset return shall be documented and acknowledged
