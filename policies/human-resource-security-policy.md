# Human Resource Security Policy

**Policy ID**: POL-HR-001
**Effective Date**: 2026-07-25
**Review Cycle**: Annually
**Owner**: Security Lead
**Classification**: Internal

## Purpose

This policy establishes security requirements for human resource processes to ensure that employees, contractors, and third parties understand their security responsibilities and are suitable for their roles.

## Scope

Applies to all personnel (employees, contractors, temporary staff, and third-party agents) who access organizational information systems or data within the Journey of Life organization.

## Policy Statements

### 1. Prior to Employment

- Background verification checks shall be conducted for all candidates who will have access to confidential or restricted data
- Verification shall be proportional to the sensitivity of the role and data access required
- Employment agreements shall include information security clauses covering:
  - Confidentiality obligations
  - Acceptable use of information assets
  - Incident reporting responsibilities
  - Consequences of policy violations
- All personnel shall receive a copy of this policy and acknowledge receipt before access is granted

### 2. During Employment

- All personnel shall complete security awareness training within 30 days of onboarding
- Security awareness training shall be refreshed annually
- Role-specific security training shall be provided when access privileges change significantly
- Personnel shall be made aware of:
  - Applicable security policies and procedures
  - Incident reporting channels and escalation paths
  - Data classification and handling requirements
  - Consequences of non-compliance
- Management shall enforce security responsibilities through:
  - Performance reviews that include security compliance
  - Recognition of security-positive behavior
  - Disciplinary process for policy violations

### 3. Termination and Change of Employment

- Access rights shall be revoked within 24 hours of employment termination
- All organizational assets shall be returned within 5 business days
- Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination per employment agreements
- Exit interviews shall include security debriefing
- For role changes:
  - Previous access rights shall be reviewed and removed if no longer required
  - New access rights shall follow the principle of least privilege
  - Access changes shall be documented and approved by the Security Lead

### 4. Contractor and Third-Party Personnel

- Contractors shall be subject to the same security requirements as employees for equivalent access levels
- Third-party agreements shall include security and confidentiality clauses per `policies/third-party-risk-policy.md`
- Contractor access shall be reviewed quarterly
- Contractor access shall be revoked within 24 hours of contract termination

### 5. Disciplinary Process

- Security policy violations shall be addressed through the organizational disciplinary process
- Violations shall be classified by severity:
  - **Minor**: First-time inadvertent violations → counseling and retraining
  - **Moderate**: Repeated or deliberate violations → formal warning
  - **Severe**: Violations causing data breach or significant risk → termination and potential legal action
- All disciplinary actions related to security shall be documented and retained for 3 years
