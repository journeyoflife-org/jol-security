# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

**Policy ID**: POL-TP-001
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Review Cycle**: Annually
**Owner**: Compliance Officer
**Classification**: Internal

## Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for assessing and managing security risks introduced by third-party vendors and service providers.

## Scope

Applies to all third parties that access, process, or store organizational data, or provide services that impact the security posture of Journey of Life.

## Policy Statements

### 1. Vendor Assessment

- All new vendors shall undergo security assessment before engagement
- Assessment shall include review of SOC 2 reports, security certifications, and incident history
- High-risk vendors require on-site or virtual security assessment
- Assessment results shall be documented using `templates/vendor-assessment-template.md`

### 2. Risk Classification

| Risk Level | Criteria                              | Review Frequency |
|------------|---------------------------------------|------------------|
| Critical   | Processes confidential/restricted data | Quarterly        |
| High       | Access to internal systems             | Semi-annually    |
| Medium     | Indirect impact on operations          | Annually         |
| Low        | No direct access or data handling      | Annually         |

### 3. Contractual Requirements

- Contracts shall include security and data protection clauses
- Right to audit shall be included in all contracts
- Breach notification requirements shall be defined (maximum 48 hours)
- Data handling and return/destruction upon termination shall be specified

### 4. Ongoing Monitoring

- Critical and high-risk vendors shall be monitored continuously
- Annual re-assessment of all active vendor relationships
- Vendor security incidents shall be tracked and assessed for organizational impact

### 5. Termination

- Access shall be revoked within 24 hours of contract termination
- Data return or destruction shall be verified
- Exit assessment shall be conducted to identify residual risks
