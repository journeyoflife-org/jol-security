# Data Retention Policy

**Policy ID**: POL-DR-001
**Effective Date**: 2026-01-01
**Review Cycle**: Annually
**Owner**: Compliance Officer
**Classification**: Internal

## Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for the retention and disposal of organizational data and records.

## Scope

Applies to all data created, received, or maintained by the Journey of Life organization.

## Policy Statements

### 1. Retention Schedules

| Data Category              | Retention Period | Legal Basis       |
|---------------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Security policies         | 3 years          | SOC 2             |
| Audit reports             | 7 years          | Regulatory        |
| Incident records          | 5 years          | SOC 2, ISO 27001  |
| Access logs               | 1 year           | SOC 2             |
| Employee records          | 7 years          | Employment law    |
| Financial records         | 7 years          | Tax law           |
| Third-party assessments   | 3 years          | SOC 2             |
| System logs               | 90 days          | Operational       |

### 2. Secure Disposal

- Electronic data shall be securely erased using approved sanitization methods
- Physical media shall be destroyed or degaussed before disposal
- Disposal records shall be maintained for 3 years

### 3. Legal Holds

- Data subject to legal hold shall not be destroyed regardless of retention period
- Legal holds are issued by Legal Counsel and tracked by Compliance Officer
- Holds shall be reviewed quarterly

### 4. Archival

- Data past active use but within retention period shall be archived
- Archives shall be encrypted and access-controlled
- Archive integrity shall be verified annually

## Enforcement

Failure to comply with retention requirements may result in regulatory penalties and legal exposure.
